# Cognitive Pressure Engine

The cognitive pressure engine detects pre-collapse systemic instability before hard failure emerges.

It tracks adaptation pressure, epistemic stress, governance saturation, contradiction density, trust fracture, identity instability, operational fatigue, uncertainty pressure, coherence decay, and survivability index.

## Invariants

- Pressure scores are deterministic and explainable.
- Pressure signals require evidence.
- High pressure degrades cognition before coherence loss.
- Critical pressure recommends fail-closed action.

## Failure Semantics

Systemic pressure uncertainty is treated as survivability risk.
