# Meta-Governance

Meta-governance governs governance itself.

It evaluates doctrine consistency, policy stability, override abuse, governance drift, constitutional pressure, and overall governance legitimacy.

## Invariants

- Governance audits are append-only.
- Doctrine conflicts require evidence.
- Governance drift must be explainable.
- Constitutional pressure can make governance illegitimate.

## Failure Semantics

Illegitimate governance cannot be trusted to authorize runtime mutation.
