# ADR 0010: Cognitive Pressure Engine

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Introduce deterministic pressure models to detect pre-collapse systemic instability.

## Rationale

The runtime can continue functioning while internal survivability collapses. Pressure metrics expose instability before failure.

## Consequences

- Pressure signals require evidence and explanation.
- Survivability index becomes a first-class read model.
- Critical systemic pressure recommends fail-closed action.
