# ADR 0011: Meta-Governance

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Add a layer that audits governance legitimacy and doctrine consistency.

## Rationale

Governance can drift, conflict, or become saturated. A self-preserving runtime must decide whether governance itself remains trustworthy.

## Consequences

- Governance legitimacy can degrade independently of execution state.
- Override abuse and doctrine inconsistency are explicit risks.
- Constitutional pressure can invalidate governance legitimacy.
